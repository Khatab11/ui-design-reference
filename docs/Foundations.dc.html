01 — Color

Roles, not decoration

One brand blue carries every primary action. It means "this is the thing to click" — so it never becomes a background wash and never emphasises body text. Everything else is a warm neutral doing structural work.

Do

Keep blue for one action per view. When two buttons sit together, only the intended next step is filled.

Don't

Don't colour text blue for emphasis — readers will try to click it. Use weight instead.

03 — Buttons

Four levels of insistence

Primary, secondary, quiet, destructive. Every button is at least 44px tall, and every one shows a visible focus ring — keyboard users buy courses too.

Label stays legible

Labels name the outcome, not the mechanism: “Enroll now”, not “Submit”. Width is content-driven — buttons never stretch to fill a container unless they are the only control in a mobile-width row.
